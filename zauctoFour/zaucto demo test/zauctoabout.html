We're building the future of procurement.

Our Mission

To democratize procurement intelligence by making AI-powered sourcing, negotiation, and supplier management accessible to every business — from startups to enterprises. We believe procurement should be proactive, not reactive.

Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted autonomous procurement platform — where AI agents handle end-to-end sourcing while human teams focus on strategic decisions. We envision a future where zero-touch procurement is the norm, not the exception.

Our Story

From a frustration to a platform trusted by thousands.

Zaucto was born from a simple observation: procurement teams spend too much time on manual sourcing, chasing quotes, and managing spreadsheets — instead of making strategic decisions.

Our founders, having spent over a decade in procurement and technology, saw an opportunity to leverage AI to transform this fragmented, inefficient process into a seamless, intelligent experience.
