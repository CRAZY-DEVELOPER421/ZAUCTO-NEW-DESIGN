Pricing FAQ

Common questions about pricing.

Can I switch plans at any time?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your dashboard. Upgrades are prorated, and downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?

Yes, all paid plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. You get full access to all features in your chosen plan during the trial.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and bank transfers for annual plans. Enterprise customers can also pay via invoice with NET 30 terms.

What does "all services and sourcing" mean in Diamond?

Diamond includes access to our managed procurement services, supplier discovery, and custom sourcing at no additional cost. This includes dedicated sourcing agents and managed RFx workflows.

Do you offer discounts for startups?

Yes! We offer a 50% discount for the first year for registered startups with less than 50 employees. Contact our sales team to apply.

What happens when I exceed my user limit?

We'll notify you when you're approaching your limit. You can either upgrade to a higher plan or purchase additional user slots at ₹1,000/user/month for your current plan tier.
