Frequently Asked Questions.

General

What is Zaucto?

Zaucto is an AI-powered procurement platform that helps businesses automate sourcing, negotiation, and supplier management. Our platform uses artificial intelligence to handle end-to-end procurement workflows — from creating RFX documents to running auctions and managing supplier relationships.

Who is Zaucto for?

Zaucto is designed for procurement teams of all sizes — from startups to large enterprises. Whether you're a procurement manager at a Fortune 500 company or a founder handling sourcing for your growing business, our platform adapts to your needs and scales with your organization.

How does AI help with procurement?

Our AI agents handle repetitive procurement tasks like creating RFPs, analyzing supplier responses, running negotiations, and identifying cost-saving opportunities. This frees up your team to focus on strategic decisions while the AI manages the operational heavy lifting.

What industries does Zaucto serve?

Zaucto serves a wide range of industries including manufacturing, IT services, construction, healthcare, retail, and energy. Our AI models are trained on diverse procurement data, making them effective across different verticals and spend categories.

Pricing & Plans

Does Zaucto offer a free trial?

Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial with full access to all features. No credit card required. You can explore the platform, run test auctions, and see how AI-powered procurement works for your business before committing to a plan.

What pricing plans do you offer?

We offer three main plans:
  • Starter — For small teams getting started with digital procurement
  • Professional — For growing companies needing advanced AI features and integrations
  • Enterprise — For large organizations requiring custom deployments, dedicated support, and SLAs
All plans include core features. Visit our pricing page for detailed comparisons.

Can I change my plan later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your dashboard. When upgrading, you'll be charged the prorated difference. When downgrading, the change takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Is there a discount for annual billing?

Yes, we offer a 20% discount when you choose annual billing. This applies to all plans and is automatically applied when you select the annual billing option during signup or upgrade.

Features

What types of RFX can I create?

Zaucto supports all major procurement document types: RFI (Request for Information), RFP (Request for Proposal), RFQ (Request for Quotation), and Bulk RFQ. Our AI can auto-generate these documents based on your requirements, saving hours of manual work.

How do live auctions work?

Our live auction feature enables real-time reverse auctions where suppliers compete by offering lower prices. You set the starting price, minimum bid decrement, and auction duration. The AI monitors bids, alerts you to anomalies, and can even auto-accept bids that meet your criteria.

Can the AI negotiate on my behalf?

Yes! Our AI Negotiation Agent can autonomously negotiate with suppliers based on your predefined parameters — target price, acceptable margins, quality requirements, and delivery timelines. You maintain full control with approval workflows and real-time visibility into every negotiation.

What analytics and reporting are available?

Zaucto provides comprehensive analytics including spend analysis, supplier performance tracking, savings reports, procurement cycle time metrics, and AI-generated insights. You can create custom dashboards, schedule automated reports, and export data in multiple formats.

Security & Compliance

Is my data secure on Zaucto?

Security is our top priority. We use AES-256 encryption for data at rest, TLS 1.3 for data in transit, and SOC 2 Type II compliant infrastructure. All data is stored in geographically redundant data centers with 99.99% uptime SLA.

What compliance standards does Zaucto meet?

Zaucto is SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and ISO 27001 certified. We also comply with regional data protection regulations including India's DPDP Act and UAE's data protection laws. Enterprise customers can request custom compliance documentation.

Can I control who sees my procurement data?

Yes, Zaucto offers granular role-based access control (RBAC). You can define roles, permissions, and data visibility rules for every team member. Suppliers only see the data you explicitly share with them, and all access is logged for audit purposes.

Do you offer data residency options?

Yes. Enterprise customers can choose their data residency region — India, UAE, Singapore, or EU. This ensures compliance with local data sovereignty requirements while maintaining full platform functionality.

Integration & API

What ERP systems does Zaucto integrate with?

Zaucto integrates with major ERP systems including SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, and NetSuite. We also support custom integrations via our REST API and webhooks. Our integration team can help set up connections to legacy systems as well.

Do you have an API?

Yes, we offer a comprehensive REST API that covers all platform functionality — from creating RFX documents to managing suppliers and running auctions. API documentation is available at docs.zaucto.com, and we provide SDKs for Python, JavaScript, and Java.

Can I connect my existing supplier database?

Absolutely. You can import your existing supplier database via CSV upload, API sync, or direct ERP integration. Our onboarding team will help migrate your supplier data and ensure all relationships, certifications, and historical data are preserved.

Does Zaucto work with procurement card systems?

Yes, Zaucto integrates with popular procurement card and expense management systems. This allows you to track spend across cards, match invoices automatically, and maintain a complete audit trail from purchase requisition to payment.
