Frequently Asked Questions

How does AI generate RFP documents?

Our AI uses advanced NLP models trained on 50,000+ procurement documents. It analyzes your requirements, cross-references with supplier capabilities, and generates a complete RFP — all in under 60 seconds.

What if I need to customize the generated RFP?

Every AI-generated RFP is fully editable. You can modify any section, add custom requirements, adjust evaluation weights, or use your own templates.

How accurate is the supplier matching?

Our ML models achieve 94% accuracy in supplier matching by analyzing capability profiles, past performance, certifications, and capacity.

Is my procurement data secure?

Absolutely. We're SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and use AES-256 encryption. Enterprise customers get dedicated instances or on-premise deployment.

Can I integrate with my existing ERP?

Yes! We offer pre-built integrations for SAP, Oracle, Tally, and 50+ other systems. Most ERP integrations are live within 24 hours.
